How the estimate works

Each confirmation represents one generation you say you avoided. We use a research benchmark of 2.907 Wh per image and estimate about 0.9 g CO₂e using that study's AWS us-west-2 grid intensity. This gross estimate does not subtract EcoLens search/network use or hardware impacts. It is not a measurement of your activity; models, image settings, and electricity mix vary.

Luccioni et al., ACM FAccT 2024
